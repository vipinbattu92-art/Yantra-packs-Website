Handheld & Fixed Reader Integrations

Trakkia works with the readers your teams already use. Fixed RFID gate readers at plant entry/exit scan assets automatically. Handheld scanners and mobile devices handle spot-checks and manual dispatch — all syncing to the same dashboard in real time.

  • ✓Fixed UHF RFID gate readers for automated gate-in/out
  • ✓Compatible with Zebra, Honeywell, and standard handheld readers
  • ✓No new infrastructure needed — integrates with existing WMS
  • ✓Offline mode on handhelds — syncs automatically on reconnect

Asset Tagging & Serial to IoT Mapping

Every asset in the Yantra Packs pool receives a unique RFID or barcode tag at manufacture. That serial number is mapped in Trakkia, creating a permanent digital twin in the cloud — so every crate, stillage, and tote has a live identity from day one.

  • ✓Unique tag-per-asset — RFID UHF or QR/barcode
  • ✓Serial mapped to IoT cloud at point of manufacture
  • ✓Grade, condition, and trip-count tracked from first scan
  • ✓Full asset provenance — custody chain across every location

Trigger Based Workflows

Every scan event in Trakkia can fire an automated action — allotment records, de-hire entries, overdue alerts, maintenance flags. No manual follow-up, no missed returns. Your supply chain runs on rules, not reminders.

  • ✓Gate-IN triggers automatic allotment to client location
  • ✓Gate-OUT triggers de-hire and return confirmation records
  • ✓Overdue threshold triggers auto-alerts to site and vendor
  • ✓Trip-count threshold triggers maintenance and retire workflow
