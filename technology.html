  • ✓Works across supplier, OEM, and Tier-2 locations

Barcode + AIDC — Works With What You Have

Not every facility has RFID infrastructure. Trakkia's AIDC module works with standard 1D/2D barcodes and QR codes — compatible with your existing handheld scanners, WMS systems, and mobile apps.

  • ✓1D barcode, 2D QR, and Data Matrix support
  • ✓Integrates with existing WMS via REST API
  • ✓Mobile scanning via Trakkia app (iOS + Android)
  • ✓Offline mode — syncs when connectivity is restored

Asset Lifecycle — Full History, Every Asset

Trakkia maintains a complete digital record for every asset — from manufacture and initial deployment through every trip, repair, and re-issue, all the way to retirement. Know the age, condition, and trip count of every item in your pool.

  • ✓Trip counter per asset (avg 200+ trips per lifecycle)
  • ✓Maintenance and repair history logged
  • ✓Grade-based asset classification (A/B/C condition)
  • ✓Automatic retire/replace triggers on degradation
