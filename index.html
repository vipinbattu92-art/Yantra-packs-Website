The Returnable Packaging Problem

Indian enterprises lose ₹1,000s of crores annually to packaging inefficiency.

💸

Hidden Costs Killing Your Margins

30–40% of packaging cost is lost in damaged, strayed, or unrecovered assets. Without visibility, losses compound every cycle.

🔍

Zero Visibility After Dispatch

Once packaging leaves your facility, it vanishes. Hoarding, delays, and non-returns erode your entire returnable asset programme.

🌍

ESG Pressure Without Solutions

BRSR, GRI, and Scope 3 mandates demand action. But most companies lack the infrastructure to measure and reduce packaging waste.
