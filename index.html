The Returnable Packaging Problem

Indian enterprises lose thousands of crores annually to untracked, unmanaged packaging.

Hidden Costs Killing Margins

30–40% of packaging cost disappears in unrecovered, damaged, or strayed assets. Without visibility, losses compound every cycle.

Zero Visibility After Dispatch

Once packaging leaves your facility it vanishes. Hoarding, delays, and non-returns erode your entire returnable asset programme.

ESG Pressure Without Solutions

BRSR, GRI, and Scope 3 mandates demand measurable action — but most companies lack the infrastructure to even track packaging carbon.

How Yantra Packs Works

A fully managed circular packaging loop — supply, track, pool, repeat.
