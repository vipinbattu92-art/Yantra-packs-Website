Hire Models

Flexible Returnable Packaging Solutions — Built Around Your Supply Chain

No capital expenditure. No asset management. Choose the hire model that fits your operation — or combine both.

🔄

Transit Hire

Packaging that moves with your goods between locations

In Transit Hire, Yantra Packs packaging circulates continuously between your suppliers, plants, and customers. Assets are tracked at every handoff — issue, transit, receipt, and return. Ideal for high-frequency, multi-location flows.

  • ✓Packaging circulates between supplier → OEM → Tier-2 → return
  • ✓Yantra manages all returns, cleaning, and redeployment
  • ✓Contract: 12–60 months, scalable volume
  • ✓Best for: Automotive Tier-1, FMCG replenishment, e-commerce returns
Recommended SKUs
Foldable CratePLS BoxFLCTotes & Utility Box
🏭

Static Hire

Packaging stationed permanently at your facility

In Static Hire, packaging is deployed at a single fixed location — your warehouse, plant floor, or distribution centre. It stays there for WIP storage, in-plant logistics, or bulk material handling throughout the contract term.

  • ✓Packaging remains at one site — no transit tracking needed
  • ✓Periodic maintenance, cleaning, and health checks by Yantra
  • ✓Contract: 12–36 months, flexible volume
  • ✓Best for: WIP storage, in-plant logistics, manufacturing lines
