About Us

We Started With a Simple Observation: India's Supply Chains Were Bleeding Packaging Costs.

In 2020, Karan Saharan and Vipin Battu founded Yantraksh Logistics to solve a problem hiding in plain sight — billions lost to untracked, unmanaged, single-use packaging.

Our Story

We saw automotive Tier-1 companies losing 30–40% of their packaging assets every year to poor visibility. FMCG companies paying for corrugated boxes that ended up in landfills. Pharma companies unable to track custody of high-value containers.

The solution wasn't just better packaging — it was a smarter system. One that tracked every asset, managed every return, and reduced carbon at the same time.

That's why we built Trakkia — India's first purpose-built RFID + barcode asset tracking platform for returnable packaging — and wrapped it with a fully managed pooling service. The result: 40% cost savings, 99.2% recovery rates, and measurable ESG impact for every client.
