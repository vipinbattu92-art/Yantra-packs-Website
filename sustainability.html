📋 Scope 3 Emissions Reduction

Packaging-related Scope 3 emissions are now reportable under BRSR (Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting). Every Yantra Packs cycle reduces your Scope 3 footprint — and Trakkia calculates it automatically.

BRSR & GRI Ready Reports

Monthly auto-generated reports aligned with BRSR Core, GRI 301 (Materials), and CDP frameworks. Share directly with your sustainability team, board, or investors — no manual data collection needed.

♻️ Mandatory 30% Recycled Plastics

India's EPR mandate requires 30% recycled content in rigid plastics from April 2025. Yantra Packs' returnable pool is built from recycled-content materials — helping you meet compliance without redesigning your packaging from scratch.
